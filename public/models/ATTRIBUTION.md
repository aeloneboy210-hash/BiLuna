# anatomy-v1.glb

Derived from:

- **Z-Anatomy — The libre 3D atlas of anatomy**, by Gauthier Kervyn and Marcin Zielinski — CC BY-SA 4.0
  https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy
- **BodyParts3D — The Database Center for Life Science** — CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan
  https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html

Changes made for BiLuna (20 November 2026): kept the skeleton and 11 organs (brain, trachea, lungs, heart chambers,
liver, stomach, duodenum and colon, pancreas, spleen, gallbladder, urinary bladder); merged each into one shape;
reduced the detail; compressed for the web. The kidneys were left out because Z-Anatomy credits its kidney model as
non-commercial only (CC BY-NC 4.0).
