# anatomy/skeleton-v1.glb and anatomy/organs-v1.glb

Derived from:

- **Z-Anatomy — The libre 3D atlas of anatomy**, by Gauthier Kervyn and Marcin Zielinski — CC BY-SA 4.0
  https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy
- **BodyParts3D — The Database Center for Life Science** — CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan
  https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html

Changes made for BiLuna (20 November 2026): kept every bone (329 parts) and the visceral organs, heart, brain regions,
eyes and spinal cord (142 parts); reduced the detail; compressed for the web. Part names and groups are in
anatomy/parts-v1.json.

These two files and parts-v1.json are shared under **CC BY-SA 4.0**: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
